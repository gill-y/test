How it works

01 · Camera nodes

Detect on the device

A phone, laptop, Raspberry Pi or IP camera runs object detection itself. Only positions are sent; video never leaves the device.

02 · Shared map

One coordinate system

No markers. The headset is the map. Each camera finds its place by watching you walk in front of it wearing the headset, and every device sees what the others detect.

03 · Headset viewer

Outlines through walls

On Quest, outlines are drawn in passthrough with depth testing off, colour-coded by camera, with distance labels, fading out when they stop updating.

Open a tool

Simulator

Fake cameras watching a fake house, on your desktop. Walk around with WASD and see people through the walls. No hardware needed.

Headset viewer

Open on your Quest from the relay server's HTTPS address and press Enter AR. The headset becomes the map.

Camera node

Turn a phone or laptop into a camera node. Calibrate it once by walking in front of it with the headset on, then leave it running.

Only connect cameras you own, pointed at places you're allowed to monitor. Every camera node shows a red "camera active" banner while it runs, and people in view should know it's there.

This page hosts the simulator and the static apps. Real cameras and headsets connect through the relay server you run on your own network (see the README).
