Printable markers

Print these and hang them flat on walls. Camera nodes use them to find their own position, and the headset uses them to line up with the cameras. Each marker prints on its own page at its exact size.

  • Print at 100% / Actual size (turn off "Fit to page"), then check the 10 cm line with a ruler. If it's off, measure the black square and type that size into the camera node.
  • Use matt paper and keep it flat. Hang each marker upright (ID text at the bottom), on a wall, ideally at about chest height.
  • Bigger is better for cameras far away: a camera should see the black square at least about 70 px wide. 15 cm works to ~2.5 m at 720p; use 19 cm (a full A4/Letter page) beyond that.
  • Use a different ID in each room. One marker per room is enough; link the rooms with the headset's Map rooms mode.
